CRITTERS VS COMPUTE

You are the forest. They are the data centre. Take it back.

Commands

  • Left click — select · drag — box select
  • Double click — select every one of that species
  • Right click — move / attack target
  • A + click — attack-move (engage on the way)
  • S — stop · H — hold position
  • W — send to water (drink for the Watered buff)
  • P / Esc — pause
  • ` — select your whole swarm
  • Shift+click a card — queue five
  • Shift+1..5 — add to a group
  • Z R X C V G N B — wolf / capybara / boar / bear / raven / porcupine / beaver / local
  • F + click — Overgrowth: root machines in an area
  • T — Deepen the Roots: raise the wildlife limit
  • Ctrl+1..5 set group · 1..5 recall
  • F1 help · F3 performance · M mute

Camera

  • Arrow keys / screen edge — pan (Shift = faster)
  • Q E — rotate · Wheel — zoom
  • Middle drag — free pan
  • Space — snap to Heart Tree
  • Click or drag the minimap to jump

How to win

  • Walk any beast onto a Grove to bloom it (biomass).
  • Every two groves adds a growing lane — more groves, more animals at once, up to three.
  • Spend biomass at the Heart Tree to grow wildlife.
  • Break the 3 Coolant Towers, then kill the Server Core.

You are the swarm

  • One wolf loses to one rifle. Four wolves do not. Never trickle animals in — mass them, then commit.
  • The Green spreads from the Heart Tree and every bloomed grove. On it your animals move faster and heal when out of combat. Off it they stay hurt.
  • Ravens fly over walls. Bears and boars break armour. Wolves are cheap enough to lose. Beavers rebuild the Heart Tree and your groves whenever they are not fighting.
  • Turrets shred massed wolves. Put Capybaras in front — the guns would rather shoot them, and they can take it.
  • Or take the power away: every turret runs off the Generator Banks. Kill them all and the guns go dark.
  • Kill the Field Technicians in orange. They weld the compound back together behind you — but only up to the amber notch on a structure's health bar. A quarter of everything you break is permanent, so an assault that dies short still leaves the next one easier.

Water

  • Intake pumps inside the compound drain the lakes. Groves in a dry catchment pay far less.
  • An animal that stops at a shore drinks and leaves Watered: +20% attack speed, +15% damage, +10% move. Fuller lake, longer buff.
  • But Watered animals do not heal — not even on the Green. Mend at home first, drink second, then commit. Drink on the way in, never on the way out.
  • Break a pump outright and its share of the drain stops for good, and the lake starts to refill.
  • Deep Wells pull groundwater, and one alone holds a third of the drain — smashing every surface intake is not the whole answer.
  • Watch the water % and the −% on your income: that is what the pumps are costing you.
  • Rain and storms refill the lakes. Break the intakes during a downpour and the water actually climbs.
  • On some sites the sky is on a clock — fronts roll through on the same schedule every attempt. Watch for raining on the water readout and time your push to it.

Reading the top bar

  • The ⚠ countdown is the next security sweep, and how many machines are already out.
  • The ×N by the queue is your growing lanes — one per two bloomed groves, up to three.
