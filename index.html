CRITTERS VS COMPUTE

You are the forest. They are the data centre. Take it back.

Commands

  • Left click — select · drag — box select
  • Double click — select all of that species on screen
  • Right click — move / attack target
  • A + click — attack-move (engage on the way)
  • S — stop · H — hold position
  • P / Esc — pause
  • Z X C V G N B — wolf / boar / bear / raven / porcupine / beaver / local
  • F + click — Overgrowth: root machines in an area
  • Ctrl+1..5 set group · 1..5 recall
  • F1 help · F3 performance · M mute

Camera

  • Arrow keys / screen edge — pan (Shift = faster)
  • Q E — rotate · Wheel — zoom
  • Middle drag — free pan
  • Space — snap to Heart Tree
  • Click or drag the minimap to jump

How to win

  • Walk any beast onto a Grove to bloom it (biomass).
  • Every grove also adds a growing lane — more groves, more animals at once, up to four.
  • Spend biomass at the Heart Tree to grow wildlife.
  • Break the 3 Coolant Towers, then kill the Server Core.

You are the swarm

  • One wolf loses to one rifle. Four wolves do not. Never trickle animals in — mass them, then commit.
  • The Green spreads from the Heart Tree and every bloomed grove. On it your animals move faster and heal when out of combat. Off it they stay hurt.
  • Ravens fly over walls. Bears and boars break armour. Wolves are cheap enough to lose. Beavers rebuild the Heart Tree and your groves whenever they are not fighting.
  • Kill the Field Technicians in orange. They weld the compound back together behind you.
